# Match-Factor non-commercial research policy

**Project mode:** non-commercial, private research and model validation.

## Permitted project activity

- Develop and evaluate original statistical models.
- Analyse fixtures for personal research and learning.
- Publish general research findings only where the underlying source licence permits publication.
- Maintain an audit trail of source, retrieval time, publication time, permitted purpose and data limitations.
- Use openly licensed datasets within their licence conditions, including required attribution.

## Excluded activity

- No paid picks, paid subscriptions, advertising, sponsorship, affiliate revenue or sale/licensing of Match-Factor outputs.
- No commercial resale, redistribution or sublicensing of third-party sports data.
- No assumption that public webpages, unofficial APIs or open-source wrappers grant rights to the underlying data.
- No automated collection, database persistence, model training or public display from a source unless its terms expressly permit that exact activity.
- No use of NBA.com statistics in connection with betting/gambling activity under the NBA.com Terms of Use, even though the project is non-commercial.
- No automated scraping/harvesting or copying NHL content into our database without written permission or another valid legal basis.
- No use of Football-Data.co.uk files for AI training or automated AI data projects; its stated policy is limited to personal betting and/or academic research with manual or light downloads.

## Source-specific gates

| Source | Match-Factor decision |
|---|---|
| Valuebetennis | May be evaluated under CC BY 4.0 with attribution and licence compliance; no bulk import until separately approved. |
| NHL official feeds | Do not automate, persist, redistribute or create derivative database records from NHL content without written permission or another valid legal basis. Non-commercial status alone is insufficient. |
| NBA.com / nba_api | Do not use NBA.com statistics for betting-related analysis, model features, training or output. The NBA Terms expressly restrict NBA Statistics from use in connection with gambling activity. Seek a provider whose licence expressly covers betting-related research and model use. |
| Football-Data.co.uk | Do not use for AI training or automated AI data collection. Its stated permitted use is personal betting and/or academic research, with manual or light downloads. |
| KHL feeds | Block automated collection and persistence until upstream rights are verified. |
| Licensed third-party providers | Use only after the contract expressly permits non-commercial research, betting-related analytics, model training, storage and intended display. |

## Governance

- This policy applies to the Match-Factor module only. It does not by itself change the commercial or non-commercial status of the wider AI-HUB platform.
- If the intended use changes, stop affected collection and re-review every provider before continuing.
- Keep a source decision log with date checked, terms URL, permitted activities, prohibited activities and reviewer notes.
- When terms are ambiguous, classify the source as `rights_unresolved` and do not automate, persist, train on or publish its data.
- Non-commercial status is a project commitment, not a workaround for copyright, contract, database rights, privacy rules or technical access restrictions.

This is an engineering policy, not legal advice. For public release or a formal legal determination, obtain advice from a qualified lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.
